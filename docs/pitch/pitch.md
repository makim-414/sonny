- **Customer:** the daughter (45 to 60) of a parent who lives alone pays. Grandma uses it. Speak to mom, and to every judge who has an aging parent.
- **Insight:** "Mom is already grandma's assistant. Sonny takes the job."
- **Language:** English. The founder's Korean grandmother is the origin story; we launch with English-speaking seniors.

## Spoken script (v4 order)

**My scene (0:00)**
This morning, I saw my mom calling a taxi for my grandmother, to the hospital. Mom is already grandma's assistant.

**The numbers (0:08)**
She is one of 63 million Americans who care for family, one in four adults, giving 27 hours a week. Families don't want to be caretakers. They want to be family. Grandma wants mom to call and say hi, not to manage her errands. Sonny takes the errands, so mom can live her day with peace of mind, and the call home is just a call home.

**The product (0:30)**
So we built Sonny, the grandkid who's always home. Grandma isn't the patient. She's the boss. One icon on her phone, and Sonny calls first. Mom loads the allowance; grandma just says it: "Do my Sunday groceries." She teaches Sonny how she likes things done, once, and Sonny does it that way from then on. When she gets stuck, Sonny doesn't make her feel slow. It quietly calls mom. Anything big or strange waits for mom's OK, and mom sees what got done, not what was said.

**The goal (1:12)**
Sonny handles the errands, so family can just be family.

**Going to market (1:16)**

## Roadmap (owner, three steps)

1. **Today: a one-icon web app on grandma's phone, set up by mom with one QR.**
2. **Next: a Sonny app on the TV she already watches all day (Android TV / Google TV, Samsung Tizen).**
3. **Then: our own Sonny device, plugged in by the family, nothing to install.** (This absorbs the earlier HDMI stick and pass-through box ideas: a box between the set-top box and the TV, drawing Sonny over live TV and driving the set-top box over HDMI-CEC or IR, is one form the device can take.)

Why this order: "The phone ships today and proves the agent; the TV apps reuse the same web app; the device comes when we know exactly what grandma uses."

Why the TV is worth it (confirmed numbers, one at a time, never added together):
- Americans 75 and older watch 4.4 hours of TV a day (BLS ATUS 2025).

## One-line answers

- **Why one icon?** She already knows exactly one gesture on her phone: tap a picture to call someone. Our whole interface is that gesture, then her voice; mom installs it and signs it in once.
- **Why TV next?** It is the one screen she watches all day, so Sonny can speak first even when the phone is in a drawer, and it is big enough for a plate number she can actually read.
- **Why now?** For the first time, what seniors know is worth teaching to AI, so being taught by grandma is a contribution she can be proud of, not a service she has to accept; and agents can finally act (book, pay, call) instead of just chat.
- **How are we different from Ato?** "Companions talk. Sonny gets things done, and calls mom." Ato is a screen-free speaker that talks and relays messages; Sonny acts for her (rides, groceries, payments), calls mom when grandma is stuck or something is strange, and grandma is the teacher, not the patient.
- **Business, one line:** "Paid by the family, monthly." (Add mom's number when we have it.)

## The hard questions (office hours) and who pays

1. **"This is a web page. How does grandma even get to it?"**
   Mom adds it to grandma's home screen as one icon and pairs it with one QR from her admin. It stays signed in; grandma never sees a login. Next, a Sonny app on the TV she already watches (Android TV / Google TV, Samsung Tizen), reusing the same web app. Then our own Sonny device, plugged in by the family, nothing to install.
2. **"Seniors don't talk to devices."**
   She talks into a phone every day; tapping the icon feels like a call, and Sonny speaks first, by name. There is nothing on screen to get wrong. And she keeps using it because it asks to be taught.
3. **"How is this different from Ato, Alexa or the voice remote she already has?"**
   Those take commands or make check-in calls. Sonny needs no new device, acts for her, pays from an allowance, calls mom before anything big, learns from her, and never shows mom what she said in private.
4. **"Who pays?"**
   The adult daughter, as a monthly family subscription, because the pain we remove is hers: the calls to check, the rides she books from her own phone. The same account funds grandma's allowance (Stripe; test mode in the demo). (Price not validated yet; if mom gives us a number today, use it.)

## Money and safety (say it when asked "isn't an agent spending money for an 80-year-old dangerous?")

- Mom tops up an allowance; grandma spends it by voice; anything big waits for mom's OK.
- Two safety layers: a strange order (quantity or amount) makes Sonny ask grandma again ("Two thousand? Not two?"). Over the per-purchase limit (default $30), Sonny calls mom: her phone rings "Sonny is calling", Sonny reads the request in its own voice (Kokoro), mom says yes or no.
- "Anything big or strange, Sonny calls mom first. Grandma keeps her independence; mom keeps the veto."
- Today the call is a full-screen ring in mom's app. Roadmap: a real phone call (telephony voice agent).
- Sonny also calls mom the other way round: "Grandma went to the temple this morning and has been quiet since lunch. You could ask about the kimchi she just made." Mom taps "Call grandma now" (Sonny tells grandma her daughter is calling) or "Remind me at 7 PM". Stage line: "Sonny doesn't replace family; it nudges mom to call, with something to talk about."

## Family admin

- Mom signs in once; taps "Connect grandma's phone"; scans one QR with grandma's phone. Done.
- Family can tune voice speed, wait time and text size from a hidden setup screen on grandma's phone (hold the top-left corner for 2 seconds, then the family PIN).
- Tabs: Today (what got done, "No need to call today", approvals), Taught (her lessons and the real counter), Routines (pills, outings, doctor, saved into her memory), Allowance (balance, history, limit, approve or decline, top up), Privacy, Device.
- Judges skip sign-in with the demo QR codes (`?demo=1`).

## Open source we build on (technical implementation)

One line for the stage: "Everything runs on open source you can self-host: Kokoro voice, Silero VAD, GBrain memory."
Full list with stars and licenses: `docs/OPEN_SOURCE.md` (every project 1,000+ stars). If asked:
- Kokoro (hexgrad/kokoro, Apache-2.0): Sonny's voice, an 82M model running on our server, no API key.
- Silero VAD (snakers4/silero-vad, MIT, via ricky0123/vad): hands-free turn taking, which is why there are no buttons.
- three.js (MIT): the single particle that is the whole interface.
- serwist (MIT): installs Sonny to the home screen like an app.
- GBrain (MIT): grandma's long-term memory with provenance, corrections and withdrawal, local PGLite.
- gstack (MIT): office hours and review during the build.
- Claude through the Vercel AI SDK: the agent loop with tool calling. The one hosted piece.

## Privacy and consent (say this whenever data comes up)

No camera. Mom sees tasks and safety, not conversations; private talk is counted, never shown, and the redaction happens on the server. Anything grandma teaches is used beyond her own agent only with her consent and her family's, and only anonymized.

## Built with GBrain and gstack (side prize)

- **GBrain** is grandma's long-term memory: every fact she teaches is stored with its source and can be corrected or withdrawn. Mom's routines go in as routine facts. Evidence: `src/lib/memory/`, `docs/gstack/`.
- **gstack** ran our office hours (diagnosis, demo strategy: "watch, don't demo", "narrow beats wide") and review. Evidence: `docs/gstack/office-hours.md`, `docs/gstack/office-hours-run.md`.
- Stage line: "Built with GBrain for grandma's long-term memory, with provenance and corrections, and gstack for office hours and review."

## Other likely questions

- **"Will she really talk to it?"** Our riskiest assumption, not tested with her yet. Our bet: she answers when it speaks first, and keeps it on because it asks to be taught.
- **"What about the real phone call?"** Today mom's app rings full screen. Next: Sonny places a real call through a telephony voice agent (pipecat or livekit, both in `docs/OPEN_SOURCE.md` as roadmap).
- **"Doesn't this replace family?"** The opposite: Sonny nudges mom to call, with something to talk about. It even rings mom when grandma has been quiet, with a topic for the call.
- **"What is the long-term vision?"** "The easiest first step into AI for the oldest generation. Then health check-ins, then the rest of their daily life." No betting or gambling.
