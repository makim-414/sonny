# Demo video script (90 seconds)

One day in grandma's life, told through one icon on her phone and mom's phone next to it.

Product name: `[NAME]` everywhere in this file. The name lives in one place, `PRODUCT_NAME_EN` in `src/lib/brand.ts`; fill it in from there when the owner decides.

The message: **a screen with nothing to learn.** No buttons, no menus. One icon, like the call button she already knows. She taps it, a single particle blooms, and the agent is already talking to her. Listening, it gathers in. Speaking, it ripples.

Rules that shape this cut:
- **Narrow beats wide** (gstack office hours): one loop is shown for real and in depth. She taps, she talks, the agent acts, she teaches it, mom's phone gets the result.
- **Her whole day** (owner): everything else is a fast, clearly labelled card montage that follows the clock.
- **Same agent, next on her TV:** the TV appears only in the roadmap at the end.

Two pillars to land:
1. Grandma hands things off to the agent: pills, taxi, groceries, her show.
2. Mom sees what got done, not what was said. She stops calling to check.

## Recording setup

- Left half: grandma's phone (`/`, the particle screen). Right half: mom's phone (`/family`). Both at phone size, side by side.
- English subtitles burned in. Korean audio stays.
- The time of day is an edit overlay in the top corner (07:50, 09:40, ...), not something the app has to show.
- Grandma's lines: recorded by a family member or read by the presenter. No real face, name or address on screen.
- Anything not live gets a small corner label: "demo data", "ride service mocked", "demo count".
- English narration only in scenes 1, 2 and 9.

## Scenes

### 1. Problem (0:00 to 0:08)

- Screen: dim living room, the TV glowing, an empty chair. Title cards:
  "My grandmother lives alone." / "My mom stays over every third night." / "Her only outing: the temple, once or twice a week." / "The rest of the day, the TV is on."
- Narration: "This is my grandmother's day. She will never learn a new app."

### 2. One icon (0:08 to 0:17)

- Screen: grandma's phone home screen, big icons. A thumb taps the `[NAME]` icon next to her call button.
- The screen goes dark, one particle blooms in the middle and ripples as it speaks:
  - Agent: "할머니, 안녕히 주무셨어요?"
  - Subtitle: "Good morning, Grandma. Did you sleep well?"
- Narration: "So she doesn't have to. No buttons. No menus. One icon, like the call button she already knows."

### 3. Morning: pills and weather (0:17 to 0:27)

- Agent: "아침 약 드셨어요? 오늘 오후에 비가 와요. 절에 가실 때 우산 챙기세요."
  - Subtitle: "Did you take your morning pills? Rain this afternoon, take an umbrella to the temple."
- The particle gathers in while she answers.
  - Grandma: "응, 방금 먹었어."
  - Subtitle: "Yes, I just took them."
- Mom's phone: the top turns green, "오늘은 전화 안 하셔도 괜찮아요", and "약: 드셨어요".
  - Subtitle on mom's side: "Mom: no need to call today."

### 4. Hospital taxi (0:27 to 0:39)

- Grandma: "오늘 병원 가는 날인데, 택시 좀 불러 줘."
  - Subtitle: "I have a hospital visit today. Call me a taxi."
- Agent: "네, 집 앞에서 병원까지 택시 불렀어요. 10분 뒤에 와요. 따님께도 알려 드렸어요."
  - Subtitle: "Done. A taxi from your door to the clinic, here in 10 minutes. I let your daughter know."
- Grandma's screen: the plate number and arrival time in very large text.
- Mom's phone: a "택시 요청" card in the feed.
- Lower third: "This morning my mom booked this exact ride for her on Kakao T, from her own phone. Now grandma just asks." Corner label: "ride service mocked".

### 5. Grandma teaches (0:39 to 0:50)

- Grandma: "내가 하나 가르쳐 줄게. 우리 딸은 사흘에 한 번 와서 자고 가. 그날은 저녁을 두 사람 몫 해야 돼."
  - Subtitle: "Let me teach you something. My daughter comes every third day and stays the night. On those days, dinner is for two."
- Agent: "가르쳐 주셔서 고마워요, 할머니. 이번 달에 저한테 37가지를 가르쳐 주셨어요."
  - Subtitle: "Thank you for teaching me, Grandma. You've taught me 37 things this month."
- A big "37" appears under the particle. Corner label: "demo count".
- Point made: she is the teacher, not the patient. What she teaches becomes the agent's long-term memory (GBrain, with source and corrections).

### 6. "Don't tell my daughter" (0:50 to 0:56)

## Live demo at the judging table (not the video)

Office hours rule: watch, don't demo. Put the phone in the judge's hand.

1. On the table: a printed card or a screen with two QR codes. "Be grandma" opens `/`. "Be her daughter" opens `/family`.
2. Judge one scans "Be grandma". The particle blooms and greets them. They just talk (Korean or English).
3. Judge two (or the presenter) scans "Be her daughter" and taps "오늘 저녁 7시에 갈게요". Grandma's phone reads it out.
4. Judge one answers. Within seconds, judge two's phone shows "할머니 답" with the summary.
5. Optional: judge one says "call me a taxi". A taxi card appears on judge two's phone.

Fallbacks: if a phone's mic is blocked, use the presenter's phone that already has the icon on its home screen. If Wi-Fi fails, phone hotspot. If both fail, play the 90 second video.
